Voice Interpreter

v2
HSK 4 · Section 1 · Verb 5

Hands-free: tap Start once, then just talk — each pause sends automatically. Say "stop" or 停 to end. Hands-free needs your Groq key.

Settings are stored separately from the main app (keys prefixed lab_), so nothing here can affect your real interpreter. With a Groq key the mic is silent (Whisper transcription). Without a key it falls back to the phone's speech recognizer.
